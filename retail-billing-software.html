Platform modules

Sell, stock, buy & report in one system

Based on QubexTrack retail workflows — optional modules (CRM, appointments, combo packs, sales commission) can be enabled per company.

Sales

POS & counter billing

  • POS when enabled — search or barcode scan, discounts, tax, payment
  • Same invoice engine as full sales; stock deducts on complete
  • Walk-in or registered customer; print receipt
  • Header POS shortcut when you have invoice-create permission
Sales

Invoices, orders & returns

  • Sales invoice — customer, warehouse, line items, tax, round-off
  • Quotation → invoice; sale order → convert to sale
  • Sale return — stock back in; payment in for receipts
  • Print, PDF, email & SMS after save (when configured)
Sales

Contacts & credit

  • Customers & suppliers — GSTIN, addresses, credit limit
  • Party due balance, history & payment allocation
  • Payment receipts — print or PDF
  • Bulk import contacts via Utilities
Inventory

Items & tracking

  • Products & services; categories, brands, HSN/SAC
  • Regular, batch (mfg/expiry), or serial / IMEI tracking
  • Opening stock, item transactions & reorder alerts
  • Import items & generate barcode labels
Inventory

Warehouse & transfers

  • Multiple warehouses; default warehouse per transaction
  • Stock transfer from → to with batch/serial lines
  • User-wise warehouse restrictions when configured
  • Stock, transfer & expired-item reports
Purchase

Supplier purchases

  • Purchase bills — stock in; cash or credit to supplier
  • Purchase order → convert to bill on receipt
  • Purchase return & payment out
  • Allocate payments to open purchase bills
Finance

Cash, bank & expenses

  • Cash in hand, cheques & bank account movements
  • Expense & income lists with categories
  • Payment types — cash, UPI, card, bank (Settings)
  • Cash flow & bank statement reports
Reports

GST & business reports

  • GSTR-1 & GSTR-2; profit and loss
  • Sale / purchase summaries; due payments
  • Item sale/purchase; batch & serial transaction reports
  • Dashboard charts, stock alerts & date filters

How teams work

Recommended retail workflows

Setup once, then run the standard sell and buy loops from the QubexTrack user manual.

Sell

Sales workflow

From quote or order to invoice, payment, and stock deduction.

  1. 1

    Quote / order

    Optional quotation or sale order for the customer.

  2. 2

    Invoice / POS

    Counter POS or full invoice — scan, tax, pay.

  3. 3

    Payment in

    Collect balance; allocate to invoices.

  5. 4

    Receipt

    Print, PDF, or notify customer.

  6. 5

    Return

    Sale return restores stock if needed.

  7. 6

    Reports

    Sales, dues & GST from Reports menu.

Buy

Purchase workflow

Restock from suppliers and keep warehouse quantities accurate.

  1. 1

    PO

    Purchase order to supplier (optional).

  2. 2

    Bill

    Purchase bill — stock increases.

  3. 3

    Transfer

    Move stock between warehouses if needed.

  5. 4

    Pay supplier

    Payment out against open bills.

  6. 5

    Return

    Purchase return adjusts stock & due.

  7. 6

    Stock check

    Reorder & stock reports on dashboard.

Data security & compliance

Enterprise-grade controls on every plan

Unlike many local desktop POS products, QubexTrack is a cloud SaaS platform with tenant isolation, audit logs, role permissions, and GST-ready compliance — shared across jewellery, restaurant, hotel & retail modules.

Security & privacy details
  • Multi-tenant isolation — separate business data, branch-level visibility
  • Audit trails — discounts, stock, voids, reprints & transfers logged
  • Role-based access — approval workflows for sensitive operations
  • Secure cloud access — TLS, backup, controlled support & subscription enforcement
  • GST compliance — invoices, GSTR reports & exportable accounts data
  • Scalable architecture — API-ready design for integrations & growth
