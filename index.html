Data security & compliance

Modern cloud ERP protection — across jewellery, restaurant, hotel & retail

QubexTrack is built as a multi-tenant cloud platform — not legacy desktop billing software. Every product shares enterprise-grade controls for data privacy, operational audit, and Indian GST compliance.

Tenant & branch isolation

Dedicated database isolation per business — critical for chains, franchises, and cloud trust. Branch-level stock and user visibility stay separated.

Audit trails & fraud controls

Activity logs for billing, discounts, stock moves, voids, reprints & transfers — so owners can trace unauthorized changes, cashier fraud, and fake returns.

Advanced role permissions

Menu-level access by role — counter staff, managers, accounts & owners see only what they need. Approval flows for sensitive actions.

Commission & staff accountability

Rule-based commission engine with reversal logic and payout workflow — especially valuable for sales-heavy showrooms and multi-staff outlets.

Cloud backup & secure access

HTTPS/TLS in transit, controlled support access, subscription enforcement, and scalable SaaS architecture — API-ready for future integrations.

GST & statutory compliance

GST-ready invoices, GSTR-1 / GSTR-3B support, and structured financial records — you remain responsible for filings; we help keep data accurate and exportable.
