Platform modules

Booking & housekeeping in one PMS

Reservations and room operations stay in sync — check-in and check-out can trigger housekeeping tasks and room status updates automatically.

Booking

Reservations & booking wizard

  • 6-step wizard: customer, dates, rooms, occupancy, review & payment
  • Standard form or wizard — branch, room search & availability
  • Filter by date range, status & booking type (upcoming/current/past)
  • Print, PDF, email & SMS from booking list
Booking

Guest lifecycle

  • Status flow: Request → Booked → Checked In → Checked Out
  • Check-in with actual timestamp; check-out releases room availability
  • Cancel, no-show, early check-in & late checkout charges
  • Housekeeping hooks on check-in / check-out when enabled
Booking

Folio & payments

  • Post charges, adjustments & payments to guest folio
  • Print folio; close folio after balance validation
  • Payment & refund history with receipt print/PDF
  • Optional Razorpay folio payment gateway
Booking

Revenue & analytics reports

  • Occupancy, ADR & RevPAR
  • Arrival, departure, in-house & no-show slices
  • Pickup & pace, cancellation trends, room-type performance
  • Folio receivables, tax & channel/source performance
Rates

Rate plans & yield

  • Rate plans with meal inclusion, refund & stay rules
  • Seasonal pricing by weekday / weekend & room type
  • Rate calendar overrides & stop-sell by date (Business)
  • Restrictions (CTA/CTD/LOS) & occupancy yield pricing (Business)
Housekeeping

Dashboard & room status board

  • KPIs: vacant dirty, occupied clean, checkout due, overdue tasks
  • Live room board with type, booking availability & assignment
  • Statuses: vacant clean/dirty, occupied, inspected, DND, out of service
  • Priority, staff assignment & audit-logged status updates
Housekeeping

Tasks & inspection

  • Tasks, My Tasks & Inspection Queue views
  • Lifecycle: pending → assigned → in progress → completed → inspected
  • Complete with consumption lines & supervisor reopen
  • SLA due dates & staff productivity reporting
Housekeeping

Inventory, linen & issue slips

  • Quick issue to staff, floor or room (HKI references)
  • Returns, wastage & manual stock adjustments
  • Linen laundry send, receive & damage write-off
  • Link slips to tasks and rooms for traceability
Housekeeping

Automation & shift reports

  • Auto checkout-clean task; vacant dirty on checkout
  • Occupied clean on check-in; stayover task rules
  • Turnaround, amenity use, wastage & low-stock snapshots
  • Export CSV; configurable SLA & priority defaults

Data security & compliance

Enterprise-grade controls on every plan

Unlike many local desktop POS products, QubexTrack is a cloud SaaS platform with tenant isolation, audit logs, role permissions, and GST-ready compliance — shared across jewellery, restaurant, hotel & retail modules.

Security & privacy details
  • Multi-tenant isolation — separate business data, branch-level visibility
  • Audit trails — discounts, stock, voids, reprints & transfers logged
  • Role-based access — approval workflows for sensitive operations
  • Secure cloud access — TLS, backup, controlled support & subscription enforcement
  • GST compliance — invoices, GSTR reports & exportable accounts data
  • Scalable architecture — API-ready design for integrations & growth
